# names-data-repo

The names of land and water, and their administrative context, as data for
the oceansensing.org ocean map: oceans, seas, straits, countries, capitals,
cities, states and provinces, counties and districts, land regions, peaks,
lakes, rivers and undersea features, each with the point it is written at, the
zoom it is named from and the state or province and country it is in; the
borders and outlines of the counties and districts those names name; and the
world's maritime boundaries, the baselines the zones are measured from
included — **not for legal, economic or navigational use** (Marine Regions'
terms).

Updated once a month.
